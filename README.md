## Hi, I'm Sankler

I build systems for the day-to-day operations of small and mid-sized businesses: deliveries, field service and sales, with a web dashboard, a mobile app and Pix payments (Brazil's instant payment system).

I start from the complaints of the people who will use the product. Before writing any code, I gather what people report on Reclame Aqui (Brazil's largest consumer complaint site), in forums and in competitor reviews, always with a source. Every feature answers one of those pain points.

### How I work

1. **Pain points with sources.** I research what bothers the people who will use the product and record each pain point with a priority.
2. **Specification.** I define clear acceptance criteria before building.
3. **Tests.** Automated tests, including security and role-based permission tests.
4. **Delivery.** I ship numbered releases, with rehearsed migrations, backups and post-release checks.
